# ALS-Related Findings from Developer Annual Reports (2009–2015)

Source: PDFs under `ALS/Annual Reports/` (Henderson Land, Kerry Properties, New World Development,
Sino Land, Sun Hong Kai Properties). Extracted from each report's Chairman's Statement /
Review of Operations / Land Bank sections.

**Scope note:** None of the five developers' annual reports use the acronym "ALS." Sino Land is the
only developer whose reports use the full phrase "Land Application List System" (twice, in FY2010 and
FY2012 Prospects commentary, as general market commentary rather than tied to a specific site). All
other developers describe government land-disposal acquisitions generically as "government land
auction" or "public/government tender" without naming the policy mechanism. Since ALS-listed sites
were disposed of by auction or tender only once triggered, and government-initiated sales (introduced
April 2011) used the same two disposal methods, annual reports generally cannot distinguish an
ALS-triggered sale from a government-initiated sale — both read identically as "auction" or "tender."
Sites awarded by the Urban Renewal Authority (URA) or MTR Corporation are a separate, non-ALS
channel and are flagged as such below.

Financial year-ends differ by company: Henderson Land, Kerry Properties and New World Development
report on a mix of calendar/June year-ends as noted; Sino Land, New World Development and Sun Hung
Kai Properties all run 1 July–30 June. Entries are grouped by the year printed on the report cover;
the actual fiscal period is noted under each entry.

## 2009

### Henderson Land — HL_2009_AR (18-month period, July 2008–Dec 2009)
- HK land bank ~19.8m sq ft attributable GFA, grown almost entirely via **agricultural land conversion**
  (Wu Kai Sha, Shatin; Lot 5369 DD116, Tai Tong Road, Yuen Long; Wo Shang Wai wetland project) and
  **old-building acquisition for redevelopment** (11 urban projects, e.g. Western Court, Matauwei
  Apartments, Hoi On Building).
- No sites acquired via government auction or tender this period.
- Mainland China additions (Chengdu, Changsha, Anshan, Dalian, Tieling) via JV purchase/bid — not an
  HK ALS-equivalent mechanism.

### Kerry Properties — KP_2009_AR (calendar year 2009)
- No new Hong Kong land bank acquisitions disclosed. HK pipeline sites (8 Muk Lun Street/Wong Tai Sin,
  Wilmer Street, Yuk Yat Street, Hing Hon Road, Shan Kwong Road, King's Road) are all urban-redevelopment/JV
  sites, not government land purchases.
- Total portfolio 50.92M sq ft attributable GFA (81% under development, 77% Mainland China).

### Sino Land — SL_2009_AR (FY July 2008–June 2009)
- Land bank ~43.5m sq ft (67% residential, 22% commercial, 5% industrial, 3% car parks, 3% hotels).
- Only addition: **Lee Tung Street/McGregor Street, Wan Chai** (Residential/Commercial, 50% JV,
  408,855 sq ft) — awarded via **Urban Renewal Authority** redevelopment rights, 23 June 2009 (not a
  government auction/tender).
- No "Application List" mention this year.

## Cross-developer synthesis

- **Terminology**: Only Sino Land's reports use the phrase "Land Application List System" verbatim
  (2010, 2012), and only as general policy commentary, never tied to a specific site acquisition. All
  five developers otherwise describe government-sourced sites simply as won via "auction" or "tender."
- **Auction → tender shift**: Across all developers, "government land auction" is the dominant term for
  acquisitions through ~2011/12, while "public/government tender" becomes near-universal from
  2012/13 onward — consistent with the April 2011 introduction of government-initiated sales (which
  used both auction and tender) and the February 2013 abolition of ALS in favour of a fully
  government-initiated Land Sale Programme. Sun Hung Kai Properties shows this most cleanly: 100% of
  its disclosed HK land-bank additions used "auction" language through FY2010/11, then shifted
  entirely to "tender" from FY2012/13 onward.
- **Henderson Land's contrarian strategy**: Henderson is the clear outlier — from 2009–2012 it
  explicitly and repeatedly stated it *avoided* auctions/tenders in favour of cheaper old-building
  redevelopment and New Territories agricultural land conversion, citing a large per-sq-ft cost
  advantage. It only began winning individual public-tender sites from FY2013 onward (Fanling 2013,
  Tsim Sha Tsui 2014, So Kwun Wat/Tuen Mun 2015) — roughly one per year — while still describing
  redevelopment/conversion as its primary channel throughout.
- **Non-ALS government-linked channels**: Urban Renewal Authority redevelopment-rights awards (Sino
  Land's Lee Tung Street 2009, KIL11200 2011, Kwun Tong Town Centre 2015; New World's Sai Yee Street
  2013) and MTR Corporation property-development tenders (New World's Austin Station 2010, Tsuen Wan
  West TW6 2013, Tai Wai Station 2015; Sino Land's Long Ping Station North 2013) recur throughout the
  period as a third and fourth land-supply channel, distinct from — but often reported alongside —
  ALS/government land sales.
